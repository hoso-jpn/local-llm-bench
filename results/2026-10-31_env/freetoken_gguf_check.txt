40-## MoE strategies
41-
42-`ft serve --moe-strategy {auto,fused,offload,cpu,hybrid}` (`--moe-backend` is the deprecated old spelling):
43-
44-- **fused** — experts resident on GPU (needs the VRAM). Serves every expert
45-  format except DeepSeek-V4's.
46-- **offload** — experts live in host RAM, an LRU cache of expert slots on GPU;
47-  misses stream over PCIe.
48-- **cpu** — misses are computed on the CPU instead of fetched.
49-- **hybrid** — per step, fetches some misses over PCIe and computes the rest on
50-  CPU, overlapped. Run `ft bench bw --model <checkpoint>` once per machine and
51-  model to calibrate the split.
52-- **auto** — dense models always resolve to `fused`; MoE models resolve to
53-  `offload`. Unified-memory GPUs differ, see below.
54-
55-### Unified-memory GPUs (GB10 / DGX Spark)
56-
